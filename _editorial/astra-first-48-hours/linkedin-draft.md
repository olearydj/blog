OpenAI released GPT-6, called Astra, and the demos appearing in the first 48 hours suggest a substantial leap in capabilities. A few that caught my eye:

- An interactive reconstruction of Waterloo, where you can pause the action and inspect the encounter from either side.
- An Onshape robot revised through requests to change brackets, improve port access, and add ventilation.
- A shooter whose streets look painted, carrying the visual style into the world you play in.
- Music composed and arranged in Ableton, with instruments and notes you can keep editing.
- New solutions to open mathematical problems, with proofs checked in Lean.

The breadth is striking, especially when the result is something you can continue working on. Demos leave plenty unanswered about reliability, cost, and how much human help went into them, but these have me reconsidering what projects are worth attempting.

# GPT-6 Astra demonstration candidates

Researched 2026-09-05. 72 distinct candidates selected from 504 unique API-captured posts plus targeted browser and primary-source research. Reposts, updates and repeated examples are consolidated. Related works by one creator remain identified as such; this is not a random or exhaustive sample.

Scores are editorial screening judgments, not measured model performance. E/D/U/N/R each use 0–5: evidence, difficulty, usefulness, originality and reproducibility. Total = 6E + 5D + 4U + 3N + 2R. A post with an attached video generally starts at E2 until inspected; a live interface can earn E4 without proving all claimed capabilities. Researcher-verified formal artifacts can earn E5, with reproduction status disclosed.

Most entries are candidates supported by a primary post, not independently verified demonstrations. Durations, autonomy, token counts and accuracy remain author claims unless the assessment says otherwise. The accompanying report provides the selected winners and deeper checks.
